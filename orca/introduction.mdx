## What is ORCA?

ORCA™ is a multi-agent AI system for teams working in Oracle Fusion Cloud (HCM, Finance, and Supply Chain). You use it to do two things against a live pod, and to ask questions about your data in between:

- **Data conversion** — migrate legacy data into Oracle Fusion. ORCA guides data preparation, validates and transforms your data, generates Oracle load files (HDL and FBDI), submits the loads, and helps you work the error cycle.
- **Configuration Workbench** — pull setup configuration out of an environment into a versioned workbook, review and edit it in a grid or in Excel, and push it into another environment.

Every read and write runs **within your own Oracle security** — as a governed Oracle call made by the connection's user. You can also ask ORCA about your Fusion data in plain language, from the app or from Claude.

## How it works, in one line

You set up a **connection** to an Oracle pod and add your **AI key**. Then you either **convert** legacy data through a **project** with **cycles** and **entities**, or use the **Configuration Workbench** to move setup between environments — while **ORCA chat** answers questions along the way.
